FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which devices are supported?

Android phones with a rear camera. The app works best on devices with multiple rear cameras. Fixed-focus lenses without autofocus cannot measure distance.

Is the app free?

Yes. It is supported by a small banner ad (Google AdMob). You can change your ad consent at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. All calculations run locally on your device. Camera images and measurement results are never stored on or sent to our servers.

How accurate is it?

It depends on your device. For everyday estimates it works well. For safety-critical or professional measurements, please use proper measuring equipment.
