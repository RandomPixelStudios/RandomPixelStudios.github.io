FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When will Facility Manager be available?

Facility Manager is coming soon to Google Play. Join our Discord to hear first when it's out.

Is the game free?

Yes. Facility Manager is free and contains no ads and no in-app tracking.

Does it need the internet or an account?

No. The game works completely offline. Your progress is stored only on your device.

Can I win or spend real money?

No. All money in the game is virtual and has no real value. The stock market and the casino are pure entertainment — not real gambling and not financial advice.

Do I keep earning while I'm away?

Yes. When you come back, the game calculates your offline earnings from the time of your last session.
