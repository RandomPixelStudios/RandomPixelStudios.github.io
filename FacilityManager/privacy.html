1. Introduction and scope

This privacy policy explains which data is processed when you play the Android game “Facility Manager”. It applies to users worldwide. The game works completely offline and locally on your device; there is no user account and no registration.

3. Data stored on your device

The game stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • your game progress: money, owned businesses, upgrades, staff, quests and stars
  • your fictional stock portfolio
  • settings (music and sound)
  • the time of your last session, used to calculate offline earnings

This data never leaves your device. We operate no servers, have no access to your data and do not share anything with third parties. The game does not require an internet connection.

4. Music, sound and screen

Music and sound effects are generated by the game on your device and can be turned off in the settings. While you play, the screen is kept on so it does not lock. None of this involves processing of personal data.

5. No ads, no tracking, no sale of data

The game contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics or tracking services and no third-party SDKs that collect personal data. We do not sell, rent or “share” (as defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act) any personal information and do not use it for targeted advertising or profiling.

6. Permissions

The game does not request any permissions. No access to the internet, camera, microphone, location, contacts or files is required.

7. Google Play and device backup

When you download the game from Google Play, Google processes data under its own responsibility and privacy policy. At most, we receive aggregated, non-personal statistics (e.g. install counts) and crash reports if you have allowed your device to send them.

If Android backup is enabled on your device, the operating system may back up game data encrypted to your Google account and restore it on reinstallation. This is controlled by your device and Google; we have no access to it.

8. Retention and deletion

Data is kept until you delete it. You can delete all data at any time with “Reset Progress” in the settings, by clearing the app data in your system settings or by uninstalling the game.

9. Legal basis

The game does not process any personal data that is transmitted to us. Data stored locally is processed only to provide the features you use (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR / UK GDPR).

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR (EU/EEA), the UK GDPR and the Swiss FADP you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, e.g. the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information Baden-Württemberg.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, e.g. under the CCPA/CPRA (California) and other U.S. state laws, the LGPD (Brazil), PIPEDA (Canada), the Privacy Act (Australia) or comparable laws. You will not be discriminated against for exercising your rights.

Since all data is stored only on your device and we have no access to it, you can view and delete it yourself at any time. For questions, contact us at the email address above.

11. International transfers

The game does not transfer any data to other countries. No data is transmitted to us or to third parties.

12. Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. Because no data is collected or transmitted, no personal information from children within the meaning of COPPA (USA) or comparable laws is sent to us or to third parties.

13. Security

Data is kept in the game's private storage, which other apps cannot access. Please protect your device with a screen lock and keep it up to date.

14. Changes

We may update this privacy policy if changes to the game or legal requirements make it necessary. The current version is always available in the game's settings and on this page.
