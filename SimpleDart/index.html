Features

Everything for a great game night.

Made for the pub, the club and the board at home — quick to start, easy to read from a distance.

301, 501 & training

Play solo or with friends, give players a handicap and choose how many darts each turn has.

Tap or type

Tap each dart right on a realistic board, or type the total of a turn on the number pad.

Voice callouts

Hear the remaining score after each turn — and the winner at the end — in your language.

Stats & ranking

Averages, best throws, 180s, win rate and an efficiency ranking for every player.

Undo & resume

Undo any throw, even after the game has ended, and pick up an interrupted game later.

Private & offline

No account, no ads, no tracking. All players, games and stats stay on your device.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When will Simple Dart be available?

Simple Dart is coming soon to Google Play. Join our Discord to hear first when it's out.

Is the app free?

Yes. Simple Dart is free and contains no ads and no in-app tracking.

Does it need the internet or an account?

No. The app works completely offline. Players, games and statistics are stored only on your device.

How do the voice callouts work?

The app uses the text-to-speech engine already installed on your phone. You can choose the language or turn callouts off in the settings. The app does not use the microphone.

Which game modes are there?

301 and 501 for any number of players (with handicaps and an adjustable number of darts per turn), plus a training mode for practising on your own.
