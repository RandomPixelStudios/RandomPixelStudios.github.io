Privacy Policy – Simple Dart

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction and scope

This privacy policy explains which data is processed when you use the Android app “Simple Dart”. It applies to users worldwide. The app works completely offline and locally on your device; there is no user account and no registration.

3. Data stored on your device

The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • player names and avatar colours you create
  • games, throws and the statistics and rankings calculated from them
  • settings (e.g. starting points, darts per turn, input method, language, sound, vibration, announcements)
  • an interrupted game, so you can resume it, and training records

The app does not transmit this data. We operate no servers, have no access to your data and do not share anything with third parties. The app does not require an internet connection. Where possible, please use nicknames as player names and only store other people's names with their consent.

4. Voice announcements (text-to-speech)

The optional announcement of the remaining score and of the winner uses the text-to-speech engine installed on your device (e.g. Google Speech Services). Only player names and scores are passed to this engine to be spoken; the app itself does not transmit any data. Depending on your device settings, the text-to-speech engine may process the text according to its own privacy terms, which are the responsibility of its provider. You can turn announcements off at any time in the app settings or directly in a game.

The app does not use the microphone and does not record or recognise speech.

5. Sound effects, vibration and screen

Sound effects are short tones generated by the app on your device. Haptic feedback uses the “vibrate” permission. Both can be turned off in the app settings. During training the screen is kept on so it does not lock while you play. None of this involves processing of personal data.

6. Sharing results

When you tap “Share” after a game, an image of the result card (with player names, date and figures) is created on your device and handed to the app you choose via the Android share menu. This only happens when you explicitly tap “Share”. The app you choose and its provider are responsible for any further processing.

7. No ads, no tracking, no sale of data

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking services and no third-party SDKs that collect personal data. We do not sell, rent or “share” (as defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act) any personal information and do not use it for targeted advertising or profiling.

8. Permissions

The app only uses the “vibrate” permission for haptic feedback. No access to the internet, camera, microphone, location, contacts or files is required. You can manage permissions at any time in your device's settings.

9. Google Play and device backup

When you download the app from Google Play, Google processes data under its own responsibility and privacy policy. At most, we receive aggregated, non-personal statistics (e.g. install counts) and crash reports if you have allowed your device to send them.

If Android backup is enabled on your device, the operating system may back up app data encrypted to your Google account and restore it on reinstallation. This is controlled by your device and Google; we have no access to it.

10. Retention and deletion

Data is kept until you delete it in the app (e.g. remove players or discard a game), uninstall the app or clear the app data in your system settings.

11. Legal basis

Where personal data (e.g. player names) is processed, this happens only locally to provide the features you request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR / UK GDPR). Processing for purely personal or household purposes may also fall outside the GDPR (Art. 2(2)(c) GDPR).

12. Your rights

Under the GDPR (EU/EEA), the UK GDPR and the Swiss FADP you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, e.g. the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information Baden-Württemberg.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, e.g. under the CCPA/CPRA (California) and other U.S. state laws, the LGPD (Brazil), PIPEDA (Canada), the Privacy Act (Australia) or comparable laws. You will not be discriminated against for exercising your rights.

Since all data is stored only on your device and we have no access to it, you can view, change and delete it yourself at any time. For questions, contact us at the email address above.

13. International transfers

The app does not transfer any data to other countries. No data is transmitted to us or to third parties.

14. Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because the app does not collect or transmit any data, no personal information from children within the meaning of COPPA (USA) or comparable laws is sent to us or to third parties. Parents can delete all data at any time in the app or by uninstalling it.

15. Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot access. Please protect your device with a screen lock and keep it up to date.

16. Changes

We may update this privacy policy if changes to the app or legal requirements make it necessary. The current version is always available in the app and on this page.
