Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

We take the protection of your personal data seriously.

This Privacy Policy explains what data may be processed when you use the Cam Distance Meter application and for what purposes.

The application is designed so that its main functions operate locally on your device.

3. Personal Data Processing

We generally do not collect or store personal data on our own servers.

The application's measurement functions are designed to operate locally on your mobile device.

We do not operate servers for storing camera data, measurement data, or user measurement data.

4. Camera Access

The application requires access to your device's camera.

Purpose

Camera access is used exclusively to provide the application's functionality for calculating and displaying distances, lengths, and other measurements.

Processing of Camera Data

Camera data used for the measurement functionality is processed locally on your device.

We do not:

  • store camera recordings on our own servers
  • transmit camera images or videos to our own servers
  • sell camera data
  • use camera data for advertising or tracking purposes

The camera is used exclusively to provide the measurement functionality requested by the user.

5. Measurement Data

Measurements and measurement results are generally processed locally on your device.

We do not have access to your measurement results and do not store them on our own servers.

If the application stores settings or other information locally on your device, such storage is used solely for the operation and functionality of the application.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To fund the application, an advertising banner is displayed at the bottom of the screen. For this we use Google AdMob, provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, and for users outside the EEA/UK/Switzerland by Google LLC, 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA ("Google").

Data Processed

When ads are loaded, Google may in particular process the following data:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • IP address
  • device type, operating system and language settings
  • approximate location (derived from the IP address)
  • ad interactions (e.g. impressions and clicks)
  • diagnostic and crash data of the ads SDK

Camera images and measurement results are never transmitted to Google or any other advertising partner.

Consent (EU/EEA, United Kingdom, Switzerland)

On first launch, a consent dialog (Google User Messaging Platform, a certified consent management platform under the IAB TCF) is shown. There you decide whether personalized ads may be shown and whether information may be stored on or accessed from your device. Without consent, only non-personalized or limited ads are served where permitted.

The legal basis is your consent pursuant to Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Section 25(1) TDDDG. You can withdraw or change your consent at any time with effect for the future: Settings → Privacy → "Privacy settings (ads)".

Under the privacy laws of certain U.S. states (e.g. California – CCPA/CPRA, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah), the disclosure of data for advertising purposes may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information or "targeted advertising". You have the right to opt out. You can do so in the app under Settings → Privacy → "Privacy settings (ads)". We do not sell personal information for money, and we do not knowingly process data of children under 16 for advertising purposes.

International Data Transfers

Google may transfer data to the United States. Google is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework; EU Standard Contractual Clauses are used in addition.

Resetting or Deleting the Advertising ID

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your Android settings under Google → Ads.

More information: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

We ourselves do not sell or rent personal data.

Apart from the advertising described in Section 6, we do not disclose personal data to third parties unless required by law or necessary for a feature explicitly used by you.
